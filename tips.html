Cyber Security Tips

🔐 Strong Passwords

Use passwords with 12+ characters, numbers, symbols and uppercase letters.

📱 Enable 2FA

Turn on Two-Factor Authentication for all important accounts.

🚫 Never Share OTPs

Never share OTPs with anyone, even if they claim to be from a bank.

📧 Beware of Phishing

Always check sender emails carefully before clicking links.

💾 Backup Your Files

Keep important files stored in cloud storage and external drives.

🛡 Keep Software Updated

Updates fix vulnerabilities and improve security protection.

🌐 Use HTTPS Websites

Enter passwords only on websites that use HTTPS.

📲 Lock Your Devices

Use passwords, PINs or biometrics to protect devices.
